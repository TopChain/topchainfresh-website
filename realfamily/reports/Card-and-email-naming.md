# Real Family file and email naming

PNG: `Category_YYYY-MM-DD_NN.png`. Dates use PT. Sequence restarts at 01 within each category each day; Vocabulary, Phrasal verbs, and Idioms & slang have 01 and 02. Other categories have 01. Category spelling: Vocabulary, Phrasal verbs, Idioms & slang, Life phrases, Grammar, Quote, Small talk. Internal lesson IDs remain stable for archive and delivery deduplication. Existing image URLs remain available for earlier emails.

Subscription daily email subject: `Real Family · Daily digest · YYYY-MM-DD · PT`. One daily message combines the recipient’s selected topics. Confirmation emails remain clearly labeled confirmation messages. Already delivered email attachments cannot be renamed retroactively.

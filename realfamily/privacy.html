Real Family

Privacy and email delivery

Updated October 8, 2026 · PT

Real Family provides news, market information, health information, recipes, and English learning materials.

Email subscriptions

When you subscribe, we collect your email address, chosen topics, confirmation status, and subscription dates. A confirmation link is required before daily subscription digests are sent. Every digest includes an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing stops future subscription digests; it does not automatically erase existing subscription or delivery records.

Private delivery records

Subscription records and email delivery status are stored in a private Neon database. Delivery records help prevent duplicate messages and investigate failures. Website requests are handled by the Vercel subscription service. Private email addresses, confirmation tokens, and delivery records are not published in the public website repositories.

Gmail access for the site owner

The private Real Family mail program requests the owner's permission to send email from topchainfresh@gmail.com and read Gmail data. Google's read-only scope can access the mailbox, but the mail program uses it to verify the sender account and search Sent messages for duplicate deliveries. Subscribers do not need to grant access to their own Gmail accounts.

Google authorization credentials are stored as private GitHub Actions secrets for unattended delivery. The owner can revoke access in their Google Account settings. Subscriber information and Gmail data are not used to train AI models or sent to lesson-generation providers by this mail program.

AI learning content

English learning content and illustrations may be generated with AI. Lesson-generation requests should contain learning topics and public lesson history, not subscriber addresses or private Gmail content.

External links

Linked news publishers, market providers, research sites, YouTube, and sharing apps operate under their own privacy policies.

Questions or deletion requests

Contact topchainfresh@gmail.com with subscription or privacy questions, or to request deletion of your subscription records. No fixed retention deadline is currently configured; unsubscribe and deletion requests are distinct actions.
